\section{Functional Requirements}
Within our project, we need to assess its ability to follow a software development life-cycle. To do so, we need to collate multitudes of papers, software engineering problems with varying difficulty and create a dataset of problems and their solutions used as benchmarks.

(1) \textbf{Requirements Engineering} - \textbf{Recall} - how many requirements were extracted

(2) \textbf{Task Decomposition} - \textbf{Number of steps in task decomposition} benchmarked to the expected number of decomposed tasks.

(3) \textbf{Software Design and Architecture} - \textbf{Number of design violations} (SOLID violations, incorrect usage of Design Patterns) 

(4) \textbf{Code Quality} - Non-functional requirements include \textbf{code readability, maintainability and reusability, SOLID violations}. Functional requirements include \textbf{number of unit tests and integration tests passed} on independent test suite.

(5) \textbf{Test Driven Development} - \textbf{Test Coverage, Test Maintenance} and ability to follow TDD. Tests include unit and integration tests.

Secondly, we want to use metrics to analyse the system . Metrics will include \textbf{Percentage of problems solved}, \textbf{Tool Correctness} (determining the correct times to call a tool). 


A successful system has the following abilities:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Understand \textbf{Software Design Principles} and execute them in appropriate scenarios,
    \item Decompose and extract \textbf{functional requirements} from context-rich problems, with a logical framework into high-level \textbf{components} and \textbf{modules} and extract \textbf{relationships} between components,
    \item \textbf{Reason} and \textbf{self-evaluate} upon initial designs and task decomposition attempts in an \textbf{iterative manner} by engaging in internal collaboration (communicating ideas between multiple agents). Provide \textbf{visibility} in internal communication and collaboration for effective debugging. Demonstrate \textbf{Test Driven Development} to produce functionally accurate software.
\end{enumerate}
